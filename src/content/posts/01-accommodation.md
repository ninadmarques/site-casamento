## In Frascati (walking or 5 minutes by car)

- **Grand Hotel Villa Tuscolana** — classic villa hotel with a garden. Booking code: **MARINAHANS27** *(placeholder)*.
- **Boutique B&Bs in the old town** — simple, charming and walkable to the main piazza.
- **Agriturismi on the Via Tuscolana** — rooms among the vineyards, usually with breakfast included.

## Nearby (10–20 minutes by car)

- **Grottaferrata** — Park Hotel Villa Grazioli, about 10 minutes from the venue.
- **Castel Gandolfo** — lake views and the Pope’s summer residence, about 20 minutes.

## Staying in Rome

If you would rather make a city holiday of it, stay near **Roma Termini**. Trains to Frascati take about 30 minutes (see *Getting There*). Plan to leave the city by early afternoon on the day.
