Think **elegant garden party in the Italian countryside**: refined, relaxed, and happily outdoors. The ceremony and dinner are formal; the setting is soft and natural.

## Our palette

We would love to see you in the colours of the hills around Frascati.

::swatches Sage #788565|Olive #424D33|Terracotta #C27D5E|Dusty rose #D9A5A0|Soft sky #A9BFD0|Wheat #D8C28F

## Please avoid

**White, ivory and cream** — those are for the bride. Very bright neon colours and all-black outfits are also best left at home.

## What to wear

- **Dresses and suits** in light fabrics (linen, silk, cotton blends). Evenings in the hills can turn cool, so bring a wrap or light jacket.
- **A hat or sunglasses** for the afternoon sun are welcome.

## Footwear

The venue has **cobblestones, gravel and grass**. Stilettos sink and slip.

- Block heels, wedges and elegant flats are your friends.
- Pack a second pair of comfortable shoes for dancing.
