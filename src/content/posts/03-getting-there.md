Frascati sits about **20 kilometres south-east of Rome**, in the Castelli Romani hills.

> Sample content: confirm timetables and shuttle details before sharing with guests.

## Airports

- **Rome Fiumicino (FCO)** — the main international airport, about 35–45 minutes by car (roughly 30 km).
- **Rome Ciampino (CIA)** — closer and used by many European low-cost airlines, about 15–20 minutes by car (roughly 12 km).

## By train

1. Get to **Roma Termini** (from FCO, the *Leonardo Express* takes about 32 minutes).
2. Take the regional train towards **Frascati** (line FL4 / Roma Termini – Frascati).
3. The journey takes **about 30 minutes**, with trains roughly every 30 minutes.
4. From Frascati station, it is a **5-minute taxi ride** or a 20-minute uphill walk to the old town.

## Taxi and car

A pre-booked taxi from FCO or CIA to Frascati costs roughly **€50–70**. If you rent a car, note that Rome’s historic centre has restricted traffic zones (**ZTL**) — avoid driving inside them.
